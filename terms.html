1Our services

We wash and repair motorhomes, fifth wheels, travel trailers and toy haulers. Washes are done at your driveway, storage lot or campsite. Some repairs need parts ordered in advance or must be done on a separate visit.

We may decline a job if the site is unsafe, the RV cannot be reached, or the work needs a specialist we do not provide. If we decline, we tell you why and you are not charged.

2Quotes and pricing

Wash prices are charged per foot of RV length, measured bumper to bumper, including the tongue or pin box on towables. The estimator on our website gives an estimate only.

  • We confirm the final price on site before starting. If the length, condition or job differs from what you told us, we tell you the new price and you can decline without charge.
  • Heavy oxidation, tree sap, paint overspray or other unusual buildup may add cost. We will tell you before doing that work.
  • Prices shown on RVsplash.com can change. The price confirmed for your job is the one you pay.

3Booking and cancelling

  • A booking is confirmed when we reply with a date and time.
  • To cancel or reschedule, tell us at least 24 hours before the appointment. Cancelling with less notice may carry a fee of $50.
  • If rain, high wind, freezing temperatures or lightning make the work unsafe or poor quality, we reschedule at no cost to you.
  • If we cannot reach the RV when we arrive (a locked gate, a blocked space, no access code), it counts as a late cancellation.

4Your site and your RV

You are responsible for making sure:

  • You have permission to have the RV washed or repaired where it is parked. Some storage lots, campgrounds and homeowner associations restrict this.
  • We have a safe working area around the RV and, where needed, access to water and power. If none is available, tell us when booking.
  • Windows, roof vents, doors and compartments are closed and latched before a wash.
  • Loose items, covers and valuables are removed from the outside of the RV.
  • You tell us about known leaks, soft spots, damaged seals or loose trim before we start.

You do not need to be present for a wash. For repairs, someone must be able to approve the quote.

5Repairs and parts

  • Repair labor is charged at $145 per hour, with a one-hour diagnostic minimum.
  • We quote parts before ordering them and do not replace anything without your approval.
  • Special-order parts may need to be paid for before we order them and may not be returnable.
  • Old parts we remove are yours to keep if you ask before the work starts. Otherwise we dispose of them.
  • Some problems are only found once work begins. If that happens, we stop and ask before doing more.

6Monthly plans

  • Plans are billed monthly at the per-foot rate for your plan, using your RV's confirmed length.
  • Every plan has a three-month minimum. After that, you can cancel any time with notice before your next billing date.
  • You can pause a plan for any month the RV is in storage or on the road. Tell us before that month's visit is scheduled.
  • Visits that are not used in a month do not carry over, unless we missed or cancelled the visit.
  • The 10% repair labor discount on Splash Total applies only while the plan is active and paid.
  • If you change RVs, the plan price changes to match the new length from the next billing date.

8Damage and claims

We photograph the RV before we start and note any damage we see. We are not responsible for conditions that existed before our visit, including:

  • Oxidation, fading, peeling decals or failing clear coat
  • Dried, cracked or failing sealant and seals
  • Loose trim, cracked fiberglass or worn awning fabric
  • Leaks that come from existing damage

If you believe we caused damage, tell us within 48 hours of the visit, with photos if you can. We will inspect it and repair it or pay for the repair if we are responsible.
