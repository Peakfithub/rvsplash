Repair

What we fix

Most problems show up while we are washing: a cracked seam, a soft spot, a seal that has dried out. We can repair it on the same visit or schedule it.

Roof and seals

Lap sealant inspection and reseal, EPDM and TPO patching, vent and skylight replacement, leak tracing.

Slide-outs

Seal replacement, alignment, motor and gear service, slide topper repair.

Awnings

Fabric replacement, arm and spring repair, motor and switch diagnosis.

Electrical

12-volt and 120-volt troubleshooting, converter and inverter replacement, house batteries, lighting, shore power cords.

Plumbing

Water pump, water heater, faucets and fittings, dump valves, tank sensors, winterizing and de-winterizing.

Appliances

Refrigerator, furnace, rooftop air conditioner and range service.

Exterior

Decal and trim replacement, compartment doors and latches, steps, minor fiberglass repair.

Pre-trip check

Roof, seals, tires and date codes, lights, propane leak test, batteries and safety detectors, with a written list of what we found.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I measure my RV?

Measure the outside, front bumper to rear bumper. For a trailer or fifth wheel, include the tongue or pin box. The length on your registration is usually close enough for a quote.

Do you need water and power at my site?

Tell us what is available when you book. If your storage lot or campground has no hookups or restricts washing, we plan the job around that.

Is the roof included?

The roof is washed in the Deep Wash and Full Detail packages. You can add a roof clean and UV treatment to any package for $3 per foot.

How often should an RV be washed?

Wash after every long trip and before storage. A rig that sits outside does best with a wash every month, which is what our monthly plans cover.

Can you wash and repair on the same day?

Usually, for small repairs such as resealing or replacing a latch. Larger jobs that need ordered parts get their own appointment.
